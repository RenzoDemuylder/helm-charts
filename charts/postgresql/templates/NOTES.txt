PostgreSQL can be accessed through the following DNS name from within your cluster:

    {{ include "common.names.fullname" . }}.{{ include "common.names.namespace" . }}.svc.{{ .Values.clusterDomain }} (port {{ .Values.service.ports.postgresql }})

To get the password for user "{{ .Values.auth.username }}" run:

    export POSTGRES_PASSWORD=$(kubectl get secret --namespace {{ include "common.names.namespace" . }} {{ include "postgresql.secretName" . }} -o jsonpath="{.data.{{ .Values.auth.secretKeys.passwordKey }}}" | base64 -d)

To connect to the database from your computer:

    kubectl port-forward --namespace {{ include "common.names.namespace" . }} svc/{{ include "common.names.fullname" . }} 5432:{{ .Values.service.ports.postgresql }} &
    PGPASSWORD="$POSTGRES_PASSWORD" psql --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5432 -U {{ .Values.auth.username }} -d {{ .Values.auth.database }}
